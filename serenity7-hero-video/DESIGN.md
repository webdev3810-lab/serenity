# Serenity properties hero video

## Style Prompt
A short photorealistic showcase of Serenity 7, Serenity 9, and Serenity 11. Each moving shot starts from a published photo of that property and uses steady, restrained camera travel with natural depth. The original homepage image remains the first hero slide; this film is the second slide. No graphics, captions, or music.

## Shot plan
- Serenity 7 exterior approach: 8 seconds from `assets/serenity-7-walk-in.mp4`.
- Serenity 9 living room: 5 seconds from `assets/serenity-9-living.mp4`.
- Serenity 11 open plan dining room: 5 seconds from `assets/serenity-11-dining.mp4`.
- Use two restrained 0.5-second dissolves for a 17-second film.

## Colors
- Cream entry columns: `#ddd0ad`
- Dark grey garage and roof: `#464644`
- Garden green: `#57753b`
- Daylight sky: `#85b8e8`

## Typography
None in the video.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not pan or zoom the reference still.
- Do not add shaky camera motion or frame vibration.
- Do not alter the house number or facade with overlays.
- Do not add text, music, or conspicuous transitions.
